CampusSignal: three symbol concepts

Each concept explores connection plus forward movement. All are code-native SVG on a 48-unit grid, shown in one colour at 140, 32, 24 and 16px on light and dark backgrounds and on the yellow app tile. Scores rate small-size clarity, distinctiveness, product relevance and fit with the rounded neubrutalist system.

01

Relay

Two path segments run toward each other and meet at a shared node, so an interrupted path becomes one line moving up and to the right. It tells the peer-help story well, but the thin strokes and tight gaps blur at 16px, and the result reads as a squiggle.

Clarity 2/5 · Distinctive 3/5 · Relevance 4/5 · System fit 3/5

02

Opening

Selected

Two solid L-shaped blocks (two learners) leave a stepped channel between them. The opening runs from the lower left to the upper right, where a signal dot shows the next step. The heavy shapes survive 16px, the silhouette is ownable, and the rounded blocks match the app's cards and radii.

Clarity 5/5 · Distinctive 4/5 · Relevance 5/5 · System fit 5/5

03

Step signal

Two rounded tiles climb toward a dot: small, achievable steps leading to progress. It is very legible, but the diagonal of squares feels generic (a progress or stairs pictogram) and doesn't express the connection between learners.

Clarity 5/5 · Distinctive 2/5 · Relevance 3/5 · System fit 4/5
