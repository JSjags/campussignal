# CampusSignal product

CampusSignal helps working open-university learners restart after life interrupts a module. MIVA learners are the first audience, not the only audience. We measure proof of a small action, not screen time or a streak.

Amaka works while studying Computer Science. She has been away from “REST, Auth & Testing a Node endpoint” for six days. Opening an entire course feels overwhelming. CampusSignal offers one confirmed outcome and a 12-minute task. The rubric is visible before she starts. A written action, connection to the outcome, and observable result earn a collectible Proof Stamp.

## Flows

1. Sign in through a Supabase magic link, or select a shared demo persona when enabled.
2. Paste an outline. Source lines are extracted deterministically. Edit, remove, and explicitly confirm outcomes. The first confirmed outcome becomes the task.
3. Start an optional 12-minute timer. Submit a short reflection and optional HTTPS photo URL. All three rubric checks award one stamp per task; further submissions do not duplicate it.
4. Opt into discovery using a neighbourhood coordinate. Find opted-in helpers within 1.6 km, filter by topic/programme, and ask for a hand.
5. The recipient accepts. Both participants can chat. Each marks the session complete. Only after both finish does the request become completed and each receive an UNSTUCK stamp.
6. Coach “stuck” suggests Phone a Friend. “DONE” followed by evidence uses the same task scorer and stamp logic.

## Boundaries

The coach does not invent course outcomes, validate academic correctness, or analyze photos. Text scoring is intentionally transparent and can be gamed; stamps are personal evidence of engagement, not credentials. The app does not scrape an LMS, promise grades, or offer jobs.

New accounts start private, with a suggested Lagos neighbourhood that must be reviewed in profile. Exact GPS is never collected. Demo accounts are shared and intentionally opted in. A help chat refreshes every five seconds. Notifications outside the open app and production moderation are future work.

## Guided setup and learning context

New accounts complete a four-step setup before entering their workspace. Optional fields can be left blank and location discovery can stay off. The next screen imports an outline or a text-based PDF, followed by explicit outcome confirmation. Home uses saved goals and study rhythm. Phone a Friend ranks opted-in helpers by topic/strength relevance and programme; the coach uses confirmed outcomes for a 12-minute plan and practice reflection prompts. Preferences can be edited from Profile or Home.
